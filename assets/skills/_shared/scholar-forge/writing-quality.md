# Writing Quality & AI-Voice Audit

Run on every draft before delivery.

## AI-voice patterns to remove

| Pattern | Replace with |
|---------|--------------|
| delve into | examine, analyze |
| landscape | field, area |
| it's important to note | (delete — state the fact) |
| remarkably / notably / importantly | (delete — show via data) |
| In conclusion, | (weave into final paragraph) |
| plays a crucial role | is required for / enables |
| leverage | use |
| holistic | (be specific) |

## Hedging calibration

- Established facts: present tense, confident
- Your new results: past tense, with uncertainty where data support it
- Speculation: "may", "suggests", "warrants further study"

## Paragraph skeleton

1. Topic sentence (claim)
2. Evidence (cite or data)
3. Analysis (so what?)
4. Transition to next paragraph

## Tense by section

| Section | Background | Your work | Others' findings |
|---------|------------|-----------|------------------|
| Introduction | present | — | past |
| Methods | — | past | — |
| Results | — | past | — |
| Discussion | present | past | past |

## Abstract checklist

- [ ] Standalone readable
- [ ] No citations (unless venue allows)
- [ ] Numbers match Results
- [ ] No undefined abbreviations
- [ ] Within word limit from venue profile

## Peer-review tone (when writing reviews)

Constructive, specific, evidence-based. Separate major vs minor issues. No ad hominem.
